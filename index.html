This Privacy Policy explains how Gurukrupa Infosoft ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application "Calculator: Hide Photos & Videos" (the "App").

We built the App to keep your private content private. The photos, videos, notes and passwords you store in the App are encrypted and kept only on your own device. We cannot see them, and they are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Please read this Policy to understand exactly what is and is not collected.

01Information We Collect

1.1 Content you store in the App (stays on your device)

The App lets you store photos, videos, notes and passwords in a private vault, protected by a PIN unlock. All of this content, together with your PIN, is:

  • encrypted on your device using strong encryption (AES-256-GCM via Google Tink for files, and an encrypted SQLCipher database for notes and passwords), with the encryption key protected by the Android Keystore;
  • stored only in the App's private storage area on your device; and
  • never transmitted to us, our servers, or any third party.
We never receive, view, or have access to your vault content or your PIN. Your PIN is stored only as a salted one-way hash on your device and cannot be recovered by us.

1.2 Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not show third-party advertisements in the App.

1.3 Anonymous analytics and diagnostic data

To understand how the App is used and to fix problems and improve it, we use Google Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google. Firebase Analytics collects anonymous, aggregated information such as:

  • app open and screen-view events and basic in-app interactions;
  • approximate session and usage statistics;
  • a randomly generated app-instance identifier;
  • general device and technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and coarse country/region (derived from IP address, which Google does not retain in analytics reports).

This analytics data is anonymous and aggregated. It never includes your photos, videos, notes, passwords, PIN, or any content stored in your vault. We use this data only to improve the App and never to identify you personally.

For more on how Google processes this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

02How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • operate and maintain the App;
  • understand which features are used so we can improve them;
  • diagnose crashes, errors, and performance issues; and
  • keep the App secure and prevent misuse.

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent any information.

03Permissions We Request and Why

The App requests only the permissions it needs for the features you use. You can grant or deny these in your device settings at any time; denying a permission may disable the related feature.

Photos and videos READ_MEDIA_IMAGES · READ_MEDIA_VIDEO · READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED · READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Used only so you can select photos and videos from your device to import into your private vault. The App reads only the items you choose. Imported media is encrypted inside the vault and is not sent anywhere.

Storage write on older devices WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below only)

Used only when you choose to export an item out of the vault back to your device.

Internet INTERNET

Used only to send the anonymous Firebase Analytics data described in Section 1.3. Your vault content is never transmitted.

04Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except as follows:

  • Google Firebase Analytics processes the anonymous analytics data described in Section 1.3 on our behalf. Google's use of that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, or in response to a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others. In practice, because your vault content never reaches us, we have no such content to disclose.

05Data Storage, Security and Retention

Your vault content is stored only on your device, encrypted as described in Section 1.1, and remains under your control. We retain no copy of it.

Anonymous analytics data is retained by Google Firebase according to Google's standard retention settings.

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your PIN and your device secure.

06Deleting Your Data

Because your vault content lives only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • You can delete individual items, notes, or passwords inside the App at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all vault content and all locally stored data from your device. This cannot be undone, so export anything you wish to keep first.

If you wish to request deletion of anonymous analytics data associated with your app instance, contact us at the email in Section 9.

07Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

08Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent.

Because the App does not collect content or account data about you, the only data involved is the anonymous analytics described in Section 1.3. To exercise any applicable right, or to ask us a question, contact us at the email in Section 9 and we will respond as required by applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

You can prevent all analytics collection by uninstalling the App.
